Info Display shows the numbers behind each fighter, the way a training lab does, and flashes a fighter the moment they can act again.

**The panel** (one per fighter: port 1, ports 1 and 2, or everyone; top corners or halfway down the sides). Pick the rows you want:

- **State and frame**: the action state and how many frames it has lasted. Green once the fighter can act: out of hitstun, past its landing lag, into a move's interruptible frames.
- **Position**, **own velocity** and **knockback velocity** (yellow while a hit is still carrying the fighter).
- **Ground speed**, and whether the fighter is grounded or airborne.
- **Jumps left** out of the character's total, and walljumps used.
- **Shield health**, out of 60.
- **Intangibility**: frames of intangibility or invincibility left (after a ledge grab or a respawn), or "(move)" when the current move makes the fighter intangible, such as a roll or a spot dodge.
- **Ledge regrab timer**: frames until the fighter can grab the ledge again.
- **Hitlag and hitstun** frames left.
- **Stick and trigger**: the stick as the game reads it (after the dead zone) and the analog L/R.

**The actionable flash.** A green ring appears on a fighter the frame they can act after being stuck: hitstun, landing lag, shield stun, a knockdown, or the end of their own move. Watch for it to learn how long your moves leave you open, or when to punish.

Press **Insert** to hide or show everything. The plugin only reads game memory; it changes nothing. It sits well with Frame Data (frame advantage after hits) and Training Lab (pause and frame advance).
