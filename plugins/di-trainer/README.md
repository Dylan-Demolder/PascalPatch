DI Trainer shows where a hit is about to send you, while you are still frozen in hitlag and can do something about it.

- The **grey** line is the path with no DI.
- The **yellow** line is the path with the DI you are holding right now. It moves with your stick until hitlag ends.
- The **green** line is the DI that survives with the most room to spare. If nothing survives, it is the DI that dies last.
- A **red X** marks where a path crosses a blast zone while you are still launched: a KO.

A small stick gauge beside the fighter shows your stick (yellow dot) and the best DI (green dot), with a verdict underneath: "Survives", "Your DI saves you", "KO: DI green to live" or "KO whatever the DI", and how many frames of hitstun the hit gives.

Press **F10** to show or hide the paths. You can turn off the grey or green line, and choose whether the paths stay on screen while you fly.

Train with it by setting a CPU on the other side and a kill percent with Training Lab's percent lock, then slow the game with F7 so hitlag lasts long enough to watch the lines move as you DI.

The prediction repeats what the game does when hitlag ends: automatic smash DI moves you 3 units along the stick if it is pushed past 0.7, then DI turns the launch by up to 18 degrees, most when the stick points across the launch. The flight uses your fighter's own gravity and fall speed and the stage's real blast zones. Both steps were checked against launches logged frame by frame in the game. Hits during the flight, platforms, walls and ceilings are not taken into account; the main floor is for Battlefield, Final Destination, Dream Land, Fountain of Dreams, Pokemon Stadium and Yoshi's Story. The plugin only reads game memory; it changes nothing.
